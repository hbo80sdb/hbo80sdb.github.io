HBO Short Takes

In between scheduled movies and programs, in addition to the promos for upcoming shows, HBO would often show short films as part of their HBO Short Take series.

These short films were quite eclectic, ranging from a couple minutes in length to ten or fifteen. Unfortunately, there's no documentation as to which ones aired and when. Luckily, Captain Quint on Letterboxd has been compiling a list of known shorts. Check out his hard work here!
